marketdhyan Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains how [Legal business name] ("marketdhyan", "we") collects and uses your personal data when you use marketdhyan.com or join our learning group. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. What we collect

  • Details you give us: name, mobile number, email address and the plan you choose.
  • Payment details: Razorpay order ID, payment ID, amount, date and status.
  • Messages: your WhatsApp number and the messages you send us (for example, your payment ID).
  • Group access: your Telegram name/username so we can add you to the group.
  • Basic technical data: your browser may store your language choice on your device. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs for security.
  • Visit statistics: Google Analytics counts visits, pages seen, device type, rough location (city) and where you came from, using cookies. We do not send your name, mobile or email to it.

2. What we never collect

We never see or store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking password. Payments are processed by Razorpay Software Private Limited under its own privacy policy.

3. Why we use your data

  • To confirm your payment and add you to the private Telegram group.
  • To contact you about your membership, renewal reminders and support.
  • To keep records required by tax and accounting law, and to prevent fraud.

We use your data only with your consent (given when you tick the boxes and pay) or where the law requires it. We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for unrelated advertising.

4. Who we share it with

Only with services needed to run the membership: Razorpay (payments), WhatsApp / Meta (messages), Telegram (group access), and our website hosting and email providers, Google Analytics (visit statistics) — and with authorities when the law requires.

5. How long we keep it

While your membership is active, and afterwards only as long as needed for tax, accounting or legal reasons (generally up to 8 years for payment records). Other data is deleted when no longer needed.

6. Your rights

  • Ask what data we hold about you, and get it corrected or updated.
  • Ask us to delete your data (we keep only what the law requires).
  • Withdraw your consent at any time — this may end your access to the group.
  • Nominate a person to exercise these rights on your behalf.
  • Complain to our Grievance Officer, and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Security

The website uses HTTPS. Payment keys and records are kept on the server, not in the browser. Only people who run the membership can see member details.

8. Children

Our membership is only for people aged 18 or above. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Grievance Officer

[Grievance Officer name] · info@marketdhyan.com · We acknowledge within 48 hours and aim to resolve within 15 days.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
