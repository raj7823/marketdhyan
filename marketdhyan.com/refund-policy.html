marketdhyan Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short: the marketdhyan membership fee is a non-refundable course fee. Refunds are given only in the three cases listed below.

1. What you are paying for

The fee gives you access to an online educational learning group (lessons, Q&A and live sessions on Telegram) for the plan period shown at the time of payment. Because access to digital learning content starts as soon as you are added, the fee is non-refundable.

2. No refund in these situations

  • You change your mind after paying, or no longer want to learn.
  • You do not use the group, miss sessions, or leave the group.
  • Your trading or investment results, profits or losses in the market.
  • You are removed from the group for breaking our Terms (for example, sharing invite links or content, spam or abuse).
  • Part of the plan period is left when you stop using it.

3. When we do refund

  • Double payment: you were charged more than once for the same plan at the same time — the extra payment is refunded.
  • Failed payment: money was deducted but the payment failed or no order was created — refunded in full (often reversed automatically by your bank or Razorpay).
  • We could not give access: we did not add you to the group within 7 days of receiving your WhatsApp message with a valid payment ID — refunded in full on request.

4. How to request

Message us on WhatsApp ([WhatsApp number for screenshots]) or email info@marketdhyan.com with your name, registered mobile number and Razorpay payment ID. We reply within 2 working days.

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method through Razorpay, usually within 5–7 working days (bank timelines may vary).

5. Cancellation

Plans do not renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel at the end of a plan. You may leave the group at any time; this does not create a right to a refund.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: info@marketdhyan.com · Grievance Officer: [Grievance Officer name] (info@marketdhyan.com).
